# Product Steering

Fudepan is a care-coordination product, not a generic AI assistant.

Its purpose is to turn handwritten household notes into safe, owned, deadline-bound care responsibilities.

## Product principles

1. The older family member should not be forced to learn a new app.
2. A caregiver may upload the note; the care recipient may review and understand it in a simple, accessible interface.
3. AI helps families understand and organize information.
4. Humans make all irreversible or high-risk decisions.
5. Responsibility must always have an owner.
6. Missing information must be surfaced, not guessed.
7. Every important action must leave an audit trail.
8. The product must make failure visible before it becomes harmful.

## Core object

The core object is not a task.

It is a Care Chain:

```text
Handwritten note
  -> understood intent
  -> structured care steps
  -> assigned responsibility
  -> human approval
  -> controlled execution
  -> evidence and timeline
```

## What Fudepan is not

Fudepan is not:

- A medical diagnosis tool
- A medication management system
- A payment automation tool
- A replacement for professional care
- An autonomous agent that acts without permission
- A generic to-do app

## Success metric

Fudepan succeeds when a family can answer:

- What needs to happen?
- Who is responsible?
- When must it happen?
- What information is still missing?
- What has been completed?
- What evidence exists?
- What will happen if nobody responds?
