# Product Steering

Fudepan is a family coordination assistant that turns handwritten household notes into safe, actionable tasks.

## Product principles

1. Meet older adults where they already are: paper, handwriting, photos, and simple language.
2. Make family members the decision-makers for anything important or irreversible.
3. Always show the original note beside AI-extracted information.
4. Prefer clarity over cleverness in UI and wording.
5. Treat accessibility as a core feature, not an afterthought.
6. Preserve a complete, understandable audit trail.

## Primary persona

- **Grandmother**, age 72, uses paper notes and a flip phone or basic smartphone.
- **Daughter**, age 42, coordinates appointments, medications, bills, and transportation.
- Both need a low-friction way to share responsibility without forcing the elder to learn a complex app.

## Success metric

A user can photograph a note and reach a reviewable task in under 30 seconds, with no sensitive action executed without explicit human approval.
